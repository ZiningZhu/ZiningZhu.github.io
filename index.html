Explainable and Controllable AI

We are the Explainable and Controllable AI Lab at Stevens Institute of Technology. We study how AI works, how to control its behavior, and how the understanding can make AIs fast, safe and reliable.

Our work connects mechanistic interpretability with reasoning, efficient computation, and AI agents for research and education.

PI: Zining Zhu (朱子宁)

From insight to impact.

Understand the mechanisms

Probe language models to connect their internal mechanisms with behavior and safety.

Build truthful AI that helps researchers and students discover and learn.

Zining Zhu (朱子宁)

Assistant Professor

Gateway South 429

zining.zhu@stevens.edu

Zining Zhu is an Assistant Professor in the School of Computing at Stevens Institute of Technology, where he directs the Explainable and Controllable AI Lab. He received his Ph.D. from the University of Toronto and Vector Institute, advised by Frank Rudzicz.

He is affiliated with SIAI and CRAFT at Stevens. His research spans natural language processing and explainable AI, connecting an understanding of model mechanisms with safer, more reliable, and efficient AI systems.

He has received a paper award at NAACL and served as Senior Area Chair for EMNLP, Area Chair for NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR, and an Action Editor for ACL Rolling Review.
